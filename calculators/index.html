About these free everyday calculators

A set of practical calculators in one place — BMI, loan and mortgage payments, percentages, age, and tip splitting. Each runs instantly in your browser with no sign-up, so you can get an answer and move on.

How to use

  1. Choose the calculator tab you need.

Why use it

Instead of finding a separate website for every calculation, these everyday tools live together in one fast page. They're handy for budgeting, quick health checks, shopping and splitting bills with friends.

FAQ

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It's a rough screening figure, not a medical diagnosis.

Does the loan calculator work for any currency?

Yes — it computes the payment from the amount, interest rate and term regardless of currency.

Are my inputs saved?

No — every calculation runs locally and nothing is stored.
