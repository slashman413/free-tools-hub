About this free unit converter

Convert between units of length, weight, temperature, area, speed and more, instantly. This converter runs in your browser, covers common metric and imperial units, and updates as you type — no redirects, no sign-up.

How to use

  1. Pick a category, such as length or weight.
  2. Enter a value and select the unit to convert from.
  3. Choose the unit to convert to and read the result instantly.

Why use it

From cooking and DIY to travel and schoolwork, you constantly need quick conversions. Having every common category on one fast page beats searching for each conversion separately.

FAQ

Which units are supported?

Common metric and imperial units across length, weight, temperature, area, speed and more.

How accurate are conversions?

They use standard exact factors; temperature uses the correct formula rather than a simple ratio.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, conversions run entirely client-side in your browser.
